ABOUT US

Our People. Our Purpose. A Healthier Tomorrow.

WHO WE ARE

The Pathology Department and Blood Bank Unit play an essential role in the diagnosis, screening, treatment and monitoring of various diseases. Our services include laboratory analysis of biological specimens such as blood, urine, tissue and other body fluids. Laboratory results help clinicians make accurate diagnoses, plan appropriate treatment and monitor patients’ health.

OUR VISION

To provide efficient, accurate and innovative pathology services through a quality management system that meets customer expectations.

OUR MISSION

To deliver efficient pathology services using up-to-date technology, supported by skilled, knowledgeable and compassionate staff who respond to customer needs and contribute to excellent patient care.

CUSTOMER CHARTER

The Pathology Unit, Hospital Dungun, is committed to providing high-quality, efficient, friendly and professional services, with fair and equitable treatment for all customers regardless of age, ethnicity, religion or socioeconomic status.

To fulfil this commitment, we pledge that:

  1. Results for urgent Full Blood Count, Renal Profile and Serum Bilirubin tests will be released within 45 minutes of specimen receipt.
  2. Blood for emergency crossmatching will be made available within 30 minutes of specimen receipt.

SAMPLE REJECTION CRITERIA

Examples and Guidelines

To ensure patient safety and accurate diagnosis, specimens that do not meet the required standards may be rejected by the laboratory.

01SPECIMEN QUALITY & COLLECTION7 criteria
  1. Insufficient specimen volume
  2. Overfilled specimen
  3. Clotted specimen
  4. Haemolysed specimen
  5. Leaking specimen container
  6. Wrong specimen container
  7. Unsatisfactory smear / specimen unsuitable for testing
02PATIENT IDENTIFICATION & LABELLING3 criteria
  1. Missing, incomplete or unclear patient name / IC / RN number
  2. Incorrect or mismatched patient identification between request form and specimen
  3. Unlabelled specimen
03REQUEST FORM & CLINICAL INFORMATION2 criteria
  1. Incorrect request form
  2. Missing, incomplete or unclear information on the request form or specimen, including clinical summary / diagnosis, specimen collection date and time, specimen information, requested test, medical officer’s name, signature or stamp, and ward / clinic information
04TEST AVAILABILITY & REQUEST ISSUES7 criteria
  1. Test not offered / unavailable
  2. Temporary reagent unavailability
  3. Equipment under maintenance
  4. Late test request
  5. Inappropriate retesting interval
  6. Additional test request (extra ordering)
  7. Specimen arrived too late for external transportation
05OTHER REJECTION REASONS4 criteria
  1. Specimen not received
  2. Repeated / duplicate specimen
  3. Test not performed / not proceeded
  4. External rejection

LABORATORY ACTIONS FOR REJECTED SPECIMENS

  1. Immediate telephone notification to the relevant ward / clinic.
  2. Document the reason for specimen rejection in iLAB.
  3. Collect a repeat specimen using the correct procedure.

Recommended Blood Collection Sequence

CLSI / KKM MALAYSIA — REFERENCE GUIDE

Blood Collection Guide — Order of Draw

The correct order of draw helps prevent additive carryover between tubes, which can cause inaccurate test results. For example, EDTA contamination may falsely increase potassium and decrease calcium results.
